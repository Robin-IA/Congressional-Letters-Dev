Letters from Congress, every Congress

Letters that members of Congress published on their own websites, preserved by the Wayback Machine. Each Congress has its own explorer, and each letter sits in exactly one: the Congress in session on the day it was written.

What the full version adds

  • Search over every explorer's index, not the one 119th index this mockup reads. Each Congress publishes its own search.json; this page loads them only as a query needs them.
  • Full-text search once the letters' text layer is indexed. Today's search matches titles, recipients, topics and members only.
  • Recipient pages across Congresses: every letter to the EPA or to the President, wherever it falls.
  • Former members: people who left before 2025 and are absent today, each with a career page.
  • Caucus and committee letters, once they are reviewed and harvested. Caucus letters are kept but not shown; committee letters are not collected yet.
